Order Book

BETA · VISUAL ORDER-TICKET EDITOR

Build a complete order plan directly on a price chart. Arrange staged entries, hard stops, trailing-stop triggers and profit targets by dragging price levels instead of filling out separate tickets.

Visual order planning

Adjust long and short order levels directly on the candlestick chart, with ticket prices alongside the chart.

Entry and exit management

Configure scaled entries, protective stops, trailing triggers and take-profit levels in one view.

IBKR connection

Connect to Interactive Brokers for market-price reference while reviewing and preparing your orders.
